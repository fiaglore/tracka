What AnchorTrakk is, and what it is not

AnchorTrakk is a personal money-tracking tool. You log income, debts, gifts, savings, expenses and investments, and it works out totals, budgets, a debt-free date and a net worth figure from what you enter. It is not a bank, it does not hold or move your money, and it does not link to your bank accounts. Bank statement import reads a file you upload, in your own browser.

Nothing in the app is investment, tax or financial advice. Every figure is plain arithmetic on numbers you typed in. You are responsible for decisions you make with it.

🔒 Privacy Policy

What we collect

Three kinds of data:

  • Account data: your email or username and sign-in identifier (Firebase Authentication), and optional profile details (name, date of birth).
  • What you enter: income, debts, gifts, savings, expenses, investments, preferences, and a push-notification address for your device if you turn notifications on. If you link a Spotify playlist, we store only its ID.
  • Payment data: if you pay, Paystack gives us a customer and subscription reference, your plan, status, renewal dates and the last four digits of your card. For bank-transfer payments we keep the receipt you upload so we can confirm your payment. We never see or store your full card number.

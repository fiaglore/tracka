How do I install it?
  1. Tap the download button above on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file AnchorTrakk.apk (it is in your notifications, or in your Downloads folder).
  3. If your phone says "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source", tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install.
  4. Open AnchorTrakk and sign in with your usual account.

The app is just AnchorTrakk opened in full screen, so it is always up to date. There is nothing to update by hand.

Is it safe? Why does my phone warn me?

Android shows that warning for any app that does not come from the Play Store. The app only opens www.anchortrakk.com.ng, asks for no special permissions, and does not link to your bank. Your data is handled exactly as in our Privacy Policy. A Google Play listing is on our list; until then you can use the download above.

Security PIN

A short PIN, set up on this device, used to: get back in after you're auto signed-out without retyping your password, confirm a plain password sign-in as an extra step, reveal anything hidden by "🙈 Hide data," and confirm deleting a logged entry. 🎵 Spotify playlist

Paste a Spotify playlist link to embed a player on your Overview page while you track. Without Spotify Premium (and being logged into Spotify in this browser), it only plays 30-second previews — that's a Spotify limitation, not AnchorTrakk's.

📌 Top bar cards

Choose which cards show in the bar at the top of every page. Turning one off just hides it — nothing is deleted, and it's back the moment you check it again.

📑 Visible tabs

Choose which tabs show in the nav bar — e.g. turn off Investments if you don't use it. Turning one off just hides it — nothing is deleted, and it's back the moment you check it again. Use the ▲▼ arrows to put them in whatever order you like. Overview always leads and What's New always trails, and neither can be reordered; XP stays fixed as the tab right before What's New too, but every other tab is free to move around it.

🎮 XP & achievements

Choose how much XP each level takes to reach. This only changes leveling speed — how you earn XP and which achievements you can unlock stay exactly the same either way.

🌍 Currency rates

💻 Trusted devices

Every device that's signed in, up to 6 at a time. Forgetting a device signs it out the next time it's opened — there's no live push for this, so it can take a moment rather than being instant. Signing in on a 7th device asks you to pick one of these to sign out first.

📜 Recent activity

The last 30 account security events — sign-ins and devices added or removed.

🔔 Notifications

Needs your browser's notification permission. Most of the types below now arrive even with the app fully closed — checked roughly every 15 minutes, so it's instant-ish rather than exact. Achievements & quests still only notify while the app is open.

📁 Data

🏦 Import a bank statement ✨ Premium

Download a statement from your own OPay, Moniepoint or Kuda app (Excel/.xlsx works best — .csv also works) and import it here. Nothing is sent anywhere — it's read right in your browser. Each transaction becomes a normal expense or income entry, exactly as if you'd typed it in yourself.

🏦 Bank statement import is a Premium feature. Upgrade in Settings to unlock it.

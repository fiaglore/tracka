📌 Top bar cards

Choose which cards show in the bar at the top of every page. Turning one off just hides it — nothing is deleted, and it's back the moment you check it again.

🎮 XP & achievements

Choose how much XP each level takes to reach. This only changes leveling speed — how you earn XP and which achievements you can unlock stay exactly the same either way.

💱 Currency & billing period

This is the default. Salary doesn't always land on the same date every month — use the "This month starts on" picker next to the month selector (🏡 Back to tracker) to override just one month at a time without changing the default for every other month.

🔐 Security

🔔 Notifications

Shows a notification for your daily reminder (if you haven't logged anything yet today), leveling up, unlocking an achievement, or fully clearing a debt. Needs your browser's notification permission. Your daily reminder, level-ups, and debt-cleared alerts now arrive even with the app fully closed — checked roughly every 15 minutes, so it's instant-ish rather than exact. Achievement unlocks still only notify while the app is open.
